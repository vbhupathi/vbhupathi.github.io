Featured work

Kumo: cloud storage as a local drive, tied to campus identity

Kumo mounts a student's or researcher's cloud storage as network drives on managed lab machines, next to the C: drive, signed in with their university account. They pick a drive letter or let Kumo choose a free one. Nothing to install on a personal device and nothing to configure at the keyboard.

The platform was first architected in 2013 and 2014, and I took it over in 2018. I have been its technical lead since, covering architecture, security, delivery, and support for a service used by 18 universities and about 400,000 students.
